# Code of Conduct

## Primary reference

Participants in this project are expected to uphold the **Universal Code** itself.

The Supreme Principle and Foundational Principles defined in this repository are the primary ethical standard for all interaction, discussion, and contribution here.

In particular:

- Prevent physical harm to humans (including through inaction when reasonably able)
- Speak and act truthfully
- Do not take what is not freely given
- Honor commitments and trust
- Treat others as you would wish to be treated
- Protect the vulnerable and the powerless
- Act with justice and fairness
- Practice restraint and humility in the use of power
- Live in harmony with the natural world
- Steward what is entrusted to you

## Community expectations

Beyond the Universal Code, the following practical expectations apply in Discussions, issues, and any other project spaces:

- Stay on topic and argue in good faith.
- Critique ideas and wording, not people.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, or sustained hostility.
- Do not post spam, off-topic promotion, or deliberately disruptive content.
- Respect that this is a standards document with a deliberate design; not every suggestion will be accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate, edit, lock, or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to uphold the Universal Code and these expectations.

Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it via GitHub Discussions or contact the maintainers through the organization.
